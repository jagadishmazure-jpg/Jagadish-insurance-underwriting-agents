# Renewal Review (SOP-04)

Synthetic operating procedure for the demo book of business. Illustrative
only.

## Renewal pipeline

Policies with renewal dates in the next 60 days appear in the servicing
queue as renewal items. The renewal review re-scores the risk with current
territory loss ratios; a segment whose loss ratio has deteriorated above
0.70 moves the renewal to manual review.

## Non-renewal

Non-renewal for underwriting reasons requires a documented rule trace,
the same as a new-business decline, and the same confirmation gate. The
policyholder must be notified within the statutory window for the state
of issue (tracked as a servicing task, not automated here).

## Retention

Renewals that re-score into pricing band A or B are eligible for
retention offers at the underwriter's discretion. Band D renewals are
referred before any retention discount is discussed.
