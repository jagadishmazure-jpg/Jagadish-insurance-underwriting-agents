# Underwriting Guidelines (SOP-01)

Synthetic operating procedure for the demo book of business. Illustrative
only; not real underwriting advice.

## Auto-approve authority

Applications may be auto-approved only when no referral rule fires. The
rules engine evaluates every application in rule-id order and records a
cited trace; an application with an empty trigger list is eligible for
straight-through processing.

## Coverage limits

Coverage above $1,000,000 always routes to a human underwriter (rule
UW-COV-01), regardless of how clean the rest of the risk looks. High-limit
property in catastrophe-exposed territories needs treaty sign-off before
binding.

## Prior claims

Three or more prior claims inside the five-year lookback window declines
the application outright (rule UW-CLM-02). One or two prior claims add to
the risk score but do not force a referral on their own.

## Pricing bands

Risk scores map to pricing bands A through D with multipliers 0.90, 1.00,
1.25, and 1.60 on the illustrative base rate. The band is advisory: the
underwriter of record sets the final rate.
